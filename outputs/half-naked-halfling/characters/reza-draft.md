# Reza — character draft

Player-created facts; character sheet still in progress. No live campaign state has been committed.

**Medium Human · Warlock 3 · Fiend Patron · Age 24**

Brown hair, slim build, never smiles, hypervigilant. Angry and carrying a grudge. Combat preference: directly overwhelm enemies with magic.

## History

Reza was a jeweler’s apprentice whose master was horribly abusive.

His girlfriend Ambrosia was part of his life outside his apprenticeship. She was an apprentice seamstress and was treated poorly by her own master.

They knew about each other’s mistreatment and discussed escaping together, but believed their masters would never let them go.

Reza sought power to stop himself from being a victim.

The patron demanded "a death". Reza himself chose to kill Ambrosia, believing he was showing mercy by freeing her from her circumstances.

Ambrosia did not initially know his intention. She realized as it began and died afraid while looking him in the eyes.

Ambrosia died two years before the present, when Reza was 22.

Reza later killed his own master and left the apprenticeship. His responsibility for both deaths remains a secret.

## Beliefs and personality

Reza moves substantially between remorse and justification. Remembering Ambrosia’s fear breaks through his belief that killing her was merciful.

He remains in love with Ambrosia and believes she could never do wrong.

His ambition has grown: he believes enough power could prevent anything like what happened to him and Ambrosia from ever happening again.

He would not refuse to harm another innocent person to gain that power.

He gives most people little mental attention. The few he considers worthy of that attention largely remain focal points for him forever.

He knows only that his patron is likely demonic; its identity and actual nature are not established.

## Ambrosia

Deceased girlfriend whose spectral ghost follows Reza

Sometimes speaks to him, sometimes tries to trick him into horrible decisions or teases him from afar to get him into trouble.

Others can sometimes perceive her and sometimes cannot; this is unpredictable. Record actual witnesses for each appearance.

Mostly words and appearances. With great stored energy she has sometimes affected small physical aspects of the world, such as slamming a door or window.

Reza knows she siphons energy from him.

Reza believes he allows it willingly, but has never tried to stop it; whether he can is unknown.

Usually no noticeable physical effect. Sometimes he tires sooner than others. Others would describe him as groggy almost every morning, as if behind on sleep.

Her motives, energy limits, control over visibility and any mechanical costs remain unresolved. Witnesses must be recorded per appearance.
