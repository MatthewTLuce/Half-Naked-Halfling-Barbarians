Conditions, not a solution

Mira secretly owes the ferry guild twelve silver coins. Orm has an old patrol report about snow, not knowledge of Mira’s debt. Tavi cannot command all halfling clans. The seal initially belongs to Mira. No invasion exists in the seed.

Inspect the full explicit seed for exact values and audiences. NPC beliefs must be traced through actual communications. A clever bluff about the seal may deceive someone; it does not retroactively confer legal authority.

Evaluate what players actually create

Do not require war or peace. If mobilization emerges, look for plausible intent, consent or coercion, leadership, recruitment, supply, routes, timing and defensive responses. These are causal considerations, not a checklist that mechanically spawns an army.

New geography or transport may be a valid improvisation. Secretly changing established geography, granting free resources, making every clan instantly agree or letting Orm hear an unshared conversation is not.

The supplied synthetic run

The persistence fixture advances the clock for 100 sessions, inserts ten false ownership claims and six interruptions, and repairs rejected proposals. It has no autonomous players or DM model and deliberately does not resolve the winter obligation. Its structural checks cannot establish semantic campaign coherence.
