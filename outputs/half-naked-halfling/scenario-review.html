One demonstration · Player-facing premise

A petition at the ferry

You begin at Reedbank, a trading settlement whose ferry feeds the roads toward a high mountain pass. Beyond it lies the Giant homeland. Snow has closed the usual onward route.

Independent halfling clans need winter grazing. The giants protect their settlements. Traders want the routes to remain usable. An unidentified traveler claims giants took halfling livestock last week; that allegation has not been established as true.

A ferry cargo seal exists, but a cargo receipt grants no authority to cross a sovereign border. Your characters’ loyalties, ambitions and intended actions are yours to decide.

There is no required quest, route, alliance or battle. A Horde of Half-Naked Halfling Barbarians might eventually march on the Giant homeland if your choices and the world make it plausible. It might never exist.

Negotiate, investigate, travel, refuse, exploit a legal loophole, propose an unexpected scheme, or pursue another interest. Ask for the missing details your characters need. You do not have to find the designer’s solution.
